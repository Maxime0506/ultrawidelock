# Code of conduct

This is a single-maintainer hobby project. The rules are short because the project is
small, not because conduct is optional.

## What is expected

Be civil and stay on the technical question. Assume the person you are replying to is
competent and acting in good faith, and say what you mean without dressing it up or
sharpening it.

Disagreement is welcome and useful. This repository is a reverse-engineering project
built against published specifications and observed behavior, so "I think that reading
of the protocol is wrong, here is my capture" is exactly the kind of exchange it needs.
Attack the claim, not the person making it.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, commit messages or reviews.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Demanding unpaid work. Response times here are best-effort and nobody is on call.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project owns: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and code review. It also applies when you are representing the project in
another venue.

## Reporting

Report privately through GitHub: **Security tab -> Report a vulnerability** on this
repository, which is the same private channel [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) uses. It is not
only for vulnerabilities and is the most reliable way to reach the maintainer without a
public thread. Do not open a public issue about someone's conduct.

Reports are read by the maintainer alone. Expect an acknowledgement rather than a fast
resolution, and expect your report to stay private unless you say otherwise.

If your report concerns the maintainer, GitHub Support is the escalation path that does
not route through the person you are reporting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete offending content, and may block accounts from the
repository. Severity and repetition decide which. There is no appeals committee here; if
you think a decision was wrong, say so through the same private channel.
