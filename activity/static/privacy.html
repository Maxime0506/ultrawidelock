Privacy Policy

openaliro Digital Twin · last updated 4 August 2026

This application collects no personal data. It is a simulator that runs entirely inside your own browser. Nothing you do in it is recorded, stored on a server, or transmitted to anyone.

What the application is

The openaliro Digital Twin is an interactive simulation of a door lock's proximity unlock logic. The decision logic is the project's own firmware compiled to WebAssembly, and it executes locally in your browser. There is no account, no login, and no server that receives anything you do.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • no personal information, and no way to submit any
  • no analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising
  • no cookies
  • no data sold, shared or disclosed to third parties, because none is held

Information the application reads but does not keep

  • Discord participant list. When run as a Discord Activity, the application asks Discord how many people currently have it open, so it can display a small "N watching" count with up to three display names. This is read, drawn on your own screen, and discarded. It is never stored and never leaves your device.
  • Theme preference. Your light or dark choice is saved in your browser's own localStorage under the key dm-theme. It stays on your device, is not readable by us, and is removed by clearing your browser's site data. If your browser blocks storage, the application still works and simply does not remember the choice.

Hosting

The static files are served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the network requests needed to deliver a page and may keep operational logs, governed by Cloudflare's own policies rather than ours. We do not receive, request or have access to those logs, and we operate no server, database or backend of our own.

Discord

When launched inside Discord, the application runs in a sandboxed frame and completes Discord's standard handshake. It requests no OAuth scopes and performs no authorisation, so it receives no access to your Discord account. Your use of Discord itself is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.

Children

The application is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Requesting deletion of your data

There is nothing held to delete. No personal data of any kind is collected or retained, so there is no record that could be produced, corrected or erased. If you believe otherwise, or want to raise any privacy question, please open an issue at github.com/openaliro/openaliro/issues and it will be answered there in public.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date above. The application's full source, including the code that backs every claim on this page, is at github.com/openaliro/openaliro.
